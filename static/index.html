Internal proof of concept

Fayda identity → wallet registry

One Fayda-verified Ethiopian identity, bound to at most one verified self-custodied wallet per chain. No custody taken, no keys held.

Mock Fayda. The identity provider here is local and not connected to the national register. The client code is real OIDC — authorization code flow, RS256 private-key-JWT client assertion — so production is an env var change. The claim names returned by userinfo are a reconstruction and are the one thing likely to need rework once you hold partner credentials.

Step 1 — verify identity

Authenticate with Fayda to establish a verified identity. In production this captures a fingerprint, iris, face or OTP.
